\subsection*{AGORA: Grassroots Barter}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  AGORA extends GB.  An instrument is a coin, a bond or an item.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacksquare$ Making.  Alice may make an Alice-item, one of a kind, which they then hold.
\item $\blacksquare$ Market.  Alice may open a market under a name of their own not used before; Alice is its keeper and first member.
\item $\blacksquare$ Joining.  Alice, the keeper of a market, may admit Bob, whom they know, by an act of both; Bob thereby agrees to come to know whoever posts to the market.
\item $\blacksquare$ Posting.  Bob, a member of a market, may post to its wall an item they hold at the time, or something they want, each at most once, and so may Alice, its keeper; the keeper sees every post on their own wall and delivers it to every other member, the poster included, who thereby knows its poster.
\item $\blacksquare$ Withdrawal.  Bob may withdraw a post of theirs; Alice, the keeper, removes it from their own wall and from the wall of every member it was delivered to, and Bob may not post it to that market again.
\item $\blacksquare$ Trading.  GB's swap and escrow apply to items as to coins and bonds; a post is not withdrawn when its item is traded.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}

\subsection*{DAO: Grassroots DAO}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice and Bob are persons.  DAO extends GKK.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacksquare$ Founding.  Alice may found a DAO under a name of their own not used before; Alice is its steward and first member.
\item $\blacksquare$ Joining.  Alice, the steward of a DAO, may admit Bob, whom they know and who is not yet a member, by an act of both.
\item $\blacksquare$ Proposing.  A member of a DAO may make a proposal to it; its steward records it and delivers it to every member but the steward, the proposer included.
\item $\blacksquare$ Voting.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote and delivers it to every member but the steward.
\item $\blacksquare$ Deciding.  The steward may decide a proposal, passed or rejected, once; the steward delivers the decision to every member but the steward.
\item $\blacksquare$ Majority.  The steward decides a proposal only once more than half of the DAO's members at that time have voted the same way on it, and passes it exactly when they voted for it.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}

\subsection*{LDAO: Liquid DAO}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice and Bob are persons.  LDAO extends DAO.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacksquare$ Delegating.  A member of a DAO, the steward included, may make another member, whom they know, their delegate in it, one at a time; the steward records the delegation.
\item $\blacksquare$ Revoking.  A member may revoke their delegation at any time; the steward erases it.
\item $\blacksquare$ Counting.  Once the delegate of a member who has not voted on a proposal has a vote on it counted, the steward counts the member as voting the same way, and the member cannot vote on it any more; so a vote passes down a chain of delegates, and a member whose chain ends with no vote, or runs into a cycle, is not counted.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}

\subsection*{FDAO: Funded DAO}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice and Bob are persons.  FDAO extends DAO and GB.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacksquare$ Funding.  A member of a DAO may give it coins they hold; its steward, who is not asked, keeps them apart as the DAO's, and they leave the DAO only as grants.
\item $\blacksquare$ Granting.  The steward may pay the proposer of a passed proposal, once per proposal, any amount of the DAO's coins of one issuer; the proposer is not asked.
\item $\circ$ Amount.  The steward grants a passed proposal the amount it asks for, as far as the DAO's coins allow.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}

\subsection*{REC: Recommendations}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  REC extends GKK.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacklozenge$ Recommending.  Alice may recommend Bob, whom they know, for something, a skill or a service, once for each thing; Bob is not asked and keeps the recommendation.
\item $\blacksquare$ Showing.  Bob may show a recommendation they hold to Carol, whom they know, once; Carol then sees who recommended Bob and for what, but does not thereby come to know the recommender.
\item $\blacksquare$ Withdrawing.  Alice may withdraw a recommendation; it is gone from Bob and from everyone Bob showed it to, and Alice may not make it again.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}

\subsection*{STAY: Grassroots Stays}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice and Bob are persons.  STAY extends GB.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacklozenge$ Hosting.  Alice, who can host guests at a place, may list it under a name not among their places.
\item $\blacksquare$ Booking.  Bob, who knows Alice, and Alice may jointly decide that Bob stays at a place of Alice's on a night later than Alice's date, for a price Bob pays in Alice-coins; a place is booked at most once a night.
\item $\blacksquare$ Cancelling.  Before the night, by Alice's date, Alice may cancel a stay, paying Bob back its whole price in Alice-coins; Bob is not asked.
\item $\blacksquare$ Reviewing.  Once the night has come, by Bob's date, Bob may review their stay, once; Alice keeps the review and is not asked.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}

\subsection*{LIB: Grassroots Library}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice and Bob are persons.  LIB extends GB.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacklozenge$ Shelving.  Alice may put a thing of their own on their shelf, under a name they have not used before.
\item $\blacksquare$ Lending.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly decide that Alice lends Bob a thing from Alice's shelf until a day later than Alice's date; it leaves the shelf, and Bob holds it but cannot lend it on.
\item $\blacksquare$ Returning.  Bob may return a borrowed thing at any time; Alice is not asked, and it goes back on Alice's shelf.
\item $\circ$ On time.  Bob returns a borrowed thing by the agreed day.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}

\subsection*{JOINT: Joint Accounts}
\begin{contractbox}
Alice and Bob are persons.  JOINT extends GC.\par\smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item $\blacksquare$ Opening.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly open a joint account, under a name of Alice's not already used for one.
\item $\blacksquare$ Depositing.  Either of them may move coins they hold into their joint account; the other is not asked.
\item $\blacksquare$ Withdrawing.  Coins leave a joint account only by an act of both, to either of them.
\end{enumerate}
\end{contractbox}
