LDAO: Liquid DAO
Alice and Bob are persons.  LDAO extends DAO.
1. ■ Delegating.  A member of a DAO may make another member, whom they know, their delegate, one at a time; the steward records it.
2. ■ Revoking.  A member may revoke their delegation at any time; the steward erases it.
3. ○ Counting.  The steward counts the vote of a member who did not vote on a proposal as their delegate's vote, and so on along the chain of delegates.
Pragmas
Delegate: my delegate in each DAO.
Delegations: the delegations Alice records.
Delegating: Bob [Delegate] <delegate>
Revoking: Bob [Revoke] <delegate>
