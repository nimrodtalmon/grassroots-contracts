STAY: Grassroots Stays
Alice and Bob are persons.  STAY extends GB.
1. ■ Hosting.  Alice may list a place, one of a kind, as their own.
2. ■ Booking.  Bob, who knows Alice, and Alice may jointly decide that Bob stays at Alice's place on a night later than Alice's date, Bob paying in Alice-coins; a place is booked at most once a night.
3. ■ Cancelling.  Before the night, Alice may cancel a stay, paying Bob back.
4. ◆ Reviewing.  After the night, Bob may review their stay, once; Alice keeps the review.
Pragmas
Places: the places Alice hosts.
Stays: the stays Alice has.
Guests: the guests Alice hosts.
Reviews: the reviews Alice received.
Hosting: Alice [List place] <place>
Booking: Bob [Book] <place> <night> <amount>
  Alice: Bob asks to stay at your place.
Cancelling: Alice [Cancel] <amount>
Reviewing: Bob [Review] <review>
