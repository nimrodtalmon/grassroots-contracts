FDAO: Funded DAO
Alice and Bob are persons.  FDAO extends DAO and GB.
1. ■ Funding.  A member of a DAO may give coins they hold to it; its steward keeps them apart, as the DAO's.
2. ■ Granting.  The steward may pay coins of the DAO to the proposer of a passed proposal, once per proposal.
3. ○ Purpose.  The steward pays out the DAO's coins only as grants to passed proposals.
Pragmas
Treasury: the dao coins Alice keeps.
Grants: the granted ones Alice paid.
Funding: Bob [Fund] <issuer> <amount>
  Alice [Fund own] <issuer> <amount>
Granting: Alice [Grant] <proposal> <issuer> <amount>
  Alice [Grant own] <proposal> <issuer> <amount>
