DAO: Grassroots DAO
Alice and Bob are persons.  DAO extends GKK.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found a DAO under a name of their own not used before; Alice is its steward and first member.
2. ■ Joining.  Alice, the steward of a DAO, may admit Bob, whom they know and who is not yet a member, by an act of both.
3. ■ Proposing.  A member of a DAO may make a proposal to it; its steward records it and delivers it to every member but the steward, the proposer included.
4. ■ Voting.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote.
5. ■ Deciding.  The steward may decide a proposal, passed or rejected, once and at any time, even before any vote; the steward delivers the decision to every member but the steward.
6. ○ Majority.  The steward decides a proposal only once more than half of the DAO's members at that time have voted the same way on it, and passes it exactly when they voted for it.
